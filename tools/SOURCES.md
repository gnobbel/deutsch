## Why these and not the ones in PLAN §4.1
- **DeReWo** (the preferred base-form frequency list from the IDS Mannheim) and **kaikki.org** (the Wiktionary extract) can't be reached from the cloud machine the agent works on, and DeReWo's license doesn't allow putting it in this public repo. The owner agreed (2026-10-28) to use the subtitle list instead. Subtitles track everyday spoken German, which suits the goal.
- The subtitle list has inflected forms (*ist, war, bin*). `rank.js` merges them into base forms using the morphology dictionaries above. A form that could belong to several base forms is shared out by how often each base form itself occurs. The result is guidance: the agent picks entries in that order, but skips names, interjections (*oh, hey, äh*), English words (*okay, Sir*), swear words, bare participles and dictionary noise (e.g. *Habe* from *habe*, *tagen* from *Tag*), and places fixed phrases next to their key word.
- Because the list is lowercased, a noun whose forms are also verb forms (*Frage/fragen*, *Stunde*, *Arbeit*, *Leben*) scores too low. The agent places such nouns by the raw count of their own forms instead. For words 1–500 this applied to Tag, Leben, Arbeit, Stunde, Frage, Ende, Land, Schule, Ding and Mal.
- **Deliberately not in words 1–500** (`tools/coverage.js` lists them): particles and pronominal adverbs that only work in context (*wohl, eben, je, bloß, ab, davon, dafür, dabei, darüber…*) come as `clozeOnly` entries with the Stage 4 bands; colloquial short forms (*raus, rein, runter, drin, dran, drauf*) are left out; *danken* and *entschuldigen* are covered by *danke* and *Entschuldigung*; *Typ, fangen, einzig, eigen, irgendwie, irgendwas* go into words 501–1000.
- **Levels:** `level` is the lowest Goethe list (A1, A2, B1) the word is on. goethe.de itself is blocked from the agent's machine, so the lists come from a GitHub copy of the official PDFs. Words not on the lists as their own entry (grammar words, phrases, Mama/Papa, film words like *töten*, *Waffe*) get a level by judgment, checked in the per-band review.
- **Learning order (`tools/order.js`):** each word keeps `freq`, its place in the cleaned frequency list above (for words 1–500 that is the order they had before 2026-11-03; for new words `rank.js`'s rank × 0.88, or the raw form rank × 0.62). `rank` is then computed, not chosen. Everyday words come before film words: the frequency is weighted by level (A1 ×0.5 … C1 ×2.2). Every A1 word is guaranteed a place in about the first 900, and A2 words follow. A short **core** list (numbers, days, months, seasons, colors, family, food, home, transport, shopping, weather, body, directions, clothes) comes within about the first 400. A word never comes before its `base`. Run `node tools/order.js` for the report (where words land, Goethe A1/A2 words still missing), and `--write` to renumber and re-split the files.
- **Goethe words deliberately not added:** inflected pronouns and pronominal adverbs (cloze material), parts of phrases we have whole (*Dank*, *Ordnung*, *Ahnung*), comparatives covered by `comparison` (*besser*), stems (*welch-*), dated items (*Fax*, *CD*, *Disco*, *Anrufbeantworter*), units and countries (*Kilo*, *Europa*), and words whose synonym is the entry (*Laden* is an `acceptAlso` of *Geschäft*, *Sonnabend* of *Samstag*), form and exam vocabulary (*Familienstand*, *ankreuzen*, *Durchsage*), forms of words we have (*Papiere*, *geöffnet*, *heraus*), and A1 words of little use to an adult learner that the A1 guarantee would otherwise push early (*Aussage, Anzeige, Lokal, zurzeit, Drucker, kulturell*…). Adjectival nouns for people (*Bekannte, Verwandte, Angestellte*) are taught with der and accept the die form. The list is `SKIP` in `tools/order.js`.
- **Verbs:** the verb dictionary has no haben/sein auxiliary, so `forms.aux` is checked in the per-band review instead of by script. The dictionary uses the old spelling (*muß*). A ß/ss-only difference is reported as "old spelling?" and then resolved by hand in `tools/crosscheck-resolved.json`.
